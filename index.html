Overview

AAB Builder lets you build signed Android App Bundles (AAB) from your phone. The build itself runs in a private repository on your own GitHub account using GitHub Actions. AAB Builder is a tool that talks to GitHub on your behalf.

Al Amin ("we", "us") does not run any server for this app. We do not receive, store or sell your source code, keystores, passwords or GitHub token.

Information stored on your device

The following is kept only on your phone:

  • Your GitHub access token, stored in encrypted storage (Android Keystore).
  • The OAuth Client ID you enter during setup, and your GitHub username.
  • Your project names and settings, recently used version codes, and cached usage figures.
  • Simple counters for ads and free builds (for example, how many builds you started today).

Keystore passwords you type are used only to create or upload a keystore and are not saved.

Information sent to GitHub

When you build, AAB Builder sends data directly from your phone to GitHub (api.github.com), using your own account:

  • Your project source, uploaded as a zip file into a private workspace repository on your account.
  • Your signing keystore and its passwords, if you set up app signing. These are encrypted on your phone (libsodium sealed box) and stored as GitHub Actions secrets in that repository.
  • Build output (the AAB), which GitHub keeps as a workflow artifact for a limited time (7 days by default).

To do this the app asks for the GitHub permissions "repo" and "workflow". Please note that these permissions apply to your GitHub account, so only build projects you trust. GitHub handles this data under its own Privacy Statement.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

AAB Builder shows ads served by Google AdMob. To get the best ads, AdMob mediation also lets other ad networks compete to show an ad; at the moment these are AppLovin and Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, Google and these networks may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Ads are not shown on the sign-in, setup or app-signing screens.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires consent, you will be asked for your choice using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy options" in the app menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · AppLovin: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/ · Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can remove ads with a one-time purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We only learn whether the purchase was completed; we never see your payment details.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics or crash-reporting service is built into the app.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Files are read only when you choose them with the system file picker (project folder, project zip or keystore).

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data leaves your phone only to (1) GitHub, at your direction, and (2) Google and its advertising partners (AppLovin, Unity Ads), for ads and purchases as described above.

Security

Your token is kept in encrypted storage, all network traffic uses HTTPS, and keystore secrets are encrypted before they leave your phone. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please protect your GitHub account (use two-factor authentication) and keep a backup of your keystore.

Retention and deletion

  • Sign out in the app to remove your token from the device.
  • Revoke the app's access any time at https://github.com/settings/applications.
  • Delete the workspace repository on GitHub to remove uploaded sources and signing secrets.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your phone.

Children

AAB Builder is a developer tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available in the app under About & privacy, and the date below changes when we do.
